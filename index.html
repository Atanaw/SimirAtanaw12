About

Research-led hydrology with practical catchment focus.

I am a hydrologist and water resources researcher with a PhD in Hydrology & Water Resources Management. My work integrates process-based hydrological modelling, field hydrology, climate analysis, GIS and remote sensing to understand runoff, erosion, floods, droughts and catchment response.

Research
